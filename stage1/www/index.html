Devlog

Project Eclipse is in production and aiming for 2027. Here is what the team has been building.

25 Sep 2026

Chapter 4 cutscenes are wired in

Trigger volumes now start each cutscene from the player's position, so the camera never jumps. Alex and Priya are polishing the transitions this week.

16 Sep 2026

Rebuilding the save system

Save states were leaking memory during long sessions. The new serializer is smaller, faster, and finally survives a four hour playthrough.

9 Sep 2026

Sound of a town without sunlight

Jonas recorded three nights of field audio for the eclipse sequence. Crickets, distant traffic, and one very confused rooster.

2 Sep 2026

Why we build everything ourselves

Every Project Eclipse build ships through Nightfall Forge, our own source and build platform. It keeps the pipeline small, fast and entirely ours.
