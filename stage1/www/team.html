The team

Nine people, one very stubborn game. We would rather ship something strange than something safe.

SW

Sam Whitlock

Studio head and producer

Sam founded Nightfall after ten years at large publishers, and keeps the schedule honest. Read Sam's studio notes.

PR

Priya Raman

Art director

Priya sets the look of every scene, from the amber corona of the eclipse to the last flickering streetlight. See her art portfolio.

AM

Alex Mercer

Lead gameplay programmer

Alex has been with Nightfall since our second project. He owns the core gameplay loop and the build pipeline for Project Eclipse. His work lives on Nightfall Forge.

JV

Jonas Vale

Audio designer

Jonas builds the soundscape from field recordings, and is still looking for the perfect creaking door. Listen to his field recording diary.

MO

Mei Okafor

Narrative designer

Mei writes the town's secrets, then hides them where only careful players will find them. Her writing samples are online.

DK

Dana Kirk

Infrastructure engineer

Dana keeps our servers, build machines and backups running. If it has a fan, she knows its name. Check the studio status page.

TR

Tomas Reyes

Gameplay designer

Tomas tunes puzzles and pacing until they are hard enough to matter and fair enough to finish. Read his puzzle design notes.

LZ

Lin Zhao

QA lead

Lin runs the weekly playtests and has broken every build we have ever called stable. Weekly playtest reports are posted here.

HI

Hana Ito

Community and press

Hana talks to players and journalists. Download the press kit or write to press@nightfall-interactive.local.
